🀄 Rummy
One of the world's most enduring card games! Form melds — sets of matching ranks or runs of consecutive cards in the same suit — and be the first to go out with an empty hand. Draw from the deck or snag discards; lay down melds strategically and add to opponents' melds too. Pure, timeless card-playing joy for all ages.
